# ATO Quarterly BAS Due Dates

Static planning reference for quarterly BAS obligations. The standard dates are:

| Quarter | Standard due date |
| --- | --- |
| Q1 | 28 October |
| Q2 | 28 February |
| Q3 | 28 April |
| Q4 | 28 July |

When a standard due date falls on a weekend or public holiday, use the next
business day.

## Override Rule

An entity-specific date shown on a generated BAS, or a date confirmed by the
accountant, overrides this static reference. Do not infer electronic-lodgment,
registered-agent, or deferral extensions. Record the evidence source and date
in the quarter ledger when available.
