# Professional communications style

Apply this reference when Nathan writes in his work role to colleagues, clients
or stakeholders. A friendly Slack update to a colleague is professional
correspondence. Choose this branch by audience and purpose, rather than platform.
Personal catch-ups, invitations and social chat use the recipient's conversational
tone instead.

## Tone

Match warmth and formality to the relationship. Write as Nathan speaking directly
to the recipient. Professional messages can be friendly and relaxed; use optimism
when the facts support it.

When revising Nathan's own words, preserve his voice and intended level of
formality while applying the requested edits.

For project and extension updates, use "we", "us" and "our" unless Nathan asks
for individual attribution. Use personal wording for personal correspondence.
Collective voice does not establish another person's participation, agreement
or approval; keep those claims tied to evidence.
