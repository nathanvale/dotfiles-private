# Transparent book memes

Use this workflow for every new Three Years of Bestie meme by default, and
for other images when Nathan requests transparency. An explicit opaque-background
choice overrides the book default. The visual guide still owns drawing, lettering, frames and native
production size; transparency does not authorize a new concept or redraw.

## Choose the route

- For a new approved meme, request true alpha transparency with the active image
  tool when the selected model and route support it. Specify a transparent outer
  field, complete readable lettering and opaque scene, panel and balloon fills.
  A painted checkerboard or approximately matching pastel is not transparency.
- For existing approved art, prefer an authorized source-preserving outer mask
  to a generative redraw. Keep the original master and make a separately named
  native-size RGBA PNG derivative. Preserve the RGB pixels; change only alpha.
  Nathan's request to remove the outer background covers this derivative.
  Respect the active tool's editing restrictions; report a preservation limit
  if its available route redraws the accepted art.
- Verify route support before promising both native resolution and transparency.
  If native alpha is unavailable, retain the full-size master and prepare an
  authorized masked derivative. Report the actual returned dimensions. Keep
  existing spending and generation approvals within their scope.

## Isolate the outer paper

Remove only paper outside the drawn panels and around free-standing captions.
Keep intentional cream speech and thought balloons, panel interiors, skin,
eyes, teeth and pale props opaque. Preserve the entire composition and caption.
Inspect faint or broken panel borders before masking: a flood fill can leak
through a tiny gap into the scene. Show any manual bridge, changed gutter or
letter-counter treatment in the proof.

Check the written PNG for real alpha, unchanged native dimensions and complete
ink, lettering and interior fills. For alpha-only derivatives, compare RGB
channels against the bound master and record their identity. Inspect edges on
the intended page colour and a contrasting colour for halos or detached specks.

## Show the result and retain acceptance

Show the full meme at its intended placement promptly. Use a readable full-size
PDF comparison for book work, with enlarged edge crops only where useful. Tiny
transparent specks are not an automatic blocker to visual review; Nathan accepts
the visible result. Report visible defects without delaying delivery for optional
microscopic refinements. Preserve accepted versions and avoid repeating their
taste decisions.

Keep the transparent derivative separate from the master and compact AI input
references. Bind accepted files to their actual dimensions and hashes before
relinking a separate InDesign candidate. Use one shared native page fill behind
the transparent field. Check each image carrier's effective fill, including
inherited object styles. Set the affected carriers locally to None when a Paper
fill would leave white rectangles behind the transparent images; preserve the
shared style and unrelated frames. Verify the exported field immediately inside
and outside each carrier against the actual page colour.
Follow the chapter's approved facing-page palette and
physical spread parity; a colour stress view does not select that page colour.
Verify the exported PDF's edges and transparency through the chosen colour
conversion. Screen approval alone does not establish a physical print match.
