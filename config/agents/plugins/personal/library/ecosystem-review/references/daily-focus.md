# Choose one daily focus

Read the shared source and authority rules in the parent skill. Refresh the
current priorities, accepted answers, open choices and actual dependencies
before recommending work. For supplied/offline records, label that boundary;
retain stale, conflicting or unread evidence and its consequences.

Ask one question at a time and wait for the answer. Skip what the conversation
already establishes. Use the following as completion criteria, not a batch
questionnaire:

1. Establish available time today.
2. Recommend one useful outcome that fits that time and evidence. Give the
   reason and let Nathan choose or propose another outcome.
3. Establish the visible finish he can point to.
4. Establish the first concrete action.
5. Check any genuine dependency: decision, evidence, access or prior work.
   Distinguish a native blocker from grouping or an architectural connection.

A blocked preferred outcome stays his preference. Explain what prevents it
and offer a useful available alternative or a prerequisite-sized action.
Wait for his choice rather than silently substituting another focus. When the
current decision frontier is unread, recommend its evidence/access repair
before treating saved choices as current. Preserve accepted decisions.

Keep a compact in-conversation distinction between confirmed answers, proposals
and unknowns. Repeat a final small summary for corrections; a clear answer or
selection confirms that field without a redundant approval round. Leave
unknowns blank on any sheet; an unanswered dependency is not "none". Optional
parking and end-of-day fields need no interview. Never infer personal answers.

End with the chosen outcome, visible finish, first action, time and any known
blocker, linked to their source. If the request also asked for a sheet, continue
through [printables](printables.md) using only confirmed answers. Otherwise end
in chat without generating an artifact.

Daily selection changes no architecture acceptance, canonical priority, issue
status, readiness, publication or execution authority. Keep unresolved design
exploration with Wayfinder, accepted outcomes needing specs/tickets with
`delivery-slice`, and requested capture/triage with `linear-intake`. Preserve the
parent skill's small Markdown handoff and actual installed-owner pointers.
