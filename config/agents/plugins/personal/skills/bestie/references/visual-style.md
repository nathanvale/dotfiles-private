## Prompt direction

Draw a warm, hand-drawn 2D cartoon with bold, slightly imperfect dark ink
outlines, simple expressive oval or dot eyes, rounded facial contours, flat
matte colour fills and subtle printed-paper grain. Use warm cream and amber,
muted teal or navy and restrained coral or rust. Keep backgrounds intentional
and drawn with the same line-and-fill technique as the characters. Use a few
simple shadow shapes and plain clothing colours with economical seam lines.
Keep the composition and embedded caption clear and legible.

Keep rendering flat rather than photographic, glossy, airbrushed or 3D.
Use simple eyes rather than oversized glassy eyes; when iris detail is shown,
preserve Nathan's blue eyes. Keep fabric and skin stylized rather than adding
realistic surface texture, reflections or cinematic depth of field.

Separate reference roles in the prompt: an edit target supplies the scene,
character details and wording; these four images supply the drawing style.
Preserve established hairstyles and clothing. In particular, keep Melanie's
ponytail unless Nathan requests a hairstyle change. Reference-only beanies,
pets, instruments and other props stay in their original reference scenes.
